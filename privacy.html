Privacy Policy

LoopCut Studio · Last updated: 23 October 2026

LoopCut Studio ("the app") is an in-house tool its operator uses to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what the app does with data from those platforms.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the operator's own accounts (account ID and username), to confirm which account it is posting to.
  • Access tokens issued by Instagram and TikTok when the operator logs in, used only to upload and publish the operator's videos.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access, collect or store data about other users, followers or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are deleted when the operator disconnects an account. The operator can revoke access at any time in the Instagram or TikTok app settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/l4mb22/loopcut-site/issues.
